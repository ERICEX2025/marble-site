Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 July 2026

Marble is an iPhone app that estimates the nutrition in your meals from a photo and coaches you toward a goal. To do that it has to handle information about your body and what you eat. This page explains exactly what is collected, who else sees it, and how to get rid of it.

The short version. Your meal photos and coach messages are sent to Anthropic to be analysed, and everything is stored in a Supabase database that only you can read. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising. Deleting your account deletes all of it.

What Marble collects

Account

  • The email address you sign up with, and a display name if you set one.

About your body

  • Profile — sex, height, activity level, goal (fat loss, muscle gain, maintain, or recomposition), and target weight.
  • Weigh-ins — the weights you record, and when.
  • Targets — the calorie and macronutrient goals Marble calculates for you, including your estimated BMR and TDEE.

What you eat

  • Meal photos you take in the app.
  • Food logs — dish name, meal type, calories, protein, carbohydrate and fat, the confidence of each estimate, and the analysis returned for that meal.
  • Weekly reviews — the summaries Marble generates from the above.

App and usage

  • Settings — theme, notification preference and time, and when your day rolls over.
  • AI usage counts — a per-account and per-device tally of AI requests, used to enforce fair-use limits and prevent abuse.

Marble does not collect your contacts, your location, or your health records, and it does not read from Apple Health.

Who else sees it

Anthropic

When you photograph a meal or message the coach, that photo or message is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which returns the nutrition estimate or reply. Anthropic processes this on Marble's behalf in order to produce that result. Marble does not use your data to train any model.

Supabase

Supabase provides the database, authentication and photo storage behind Marble, hosted in the United States. Access is restricted per user at the database level: your rows and your photos are readable only by your own account.

There are no advertising networks, no data brokers, and no third-party trackers in Marble. Your information is never sold.

How long it's kept

Your logs and photos stay until you delete them or delete your account. Deleting a meal removes that entry and its photo. There is no separate archive kept after deletion.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account from within the app, under Settings. This permanently removes your profile, weigh-ins, food logs, meal photos, targets and reviews. It cannot be undone.

If you would rather it be done for you, or you can no longer sign in, email the address below and it will be handled.

Children

Marble is not intended for children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and it will be removed.

Not medical advice

Marble estimates nutrition and suggests targets. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian before making significant changes to how you eat, particularly if you have a health condition or a history of disordered eating.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who it is shared with, the date at the top will change and the app will say so.
