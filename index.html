How it works

Three passes, the way stone gets worked.

I

Set the block

A short conversation about your height, weight, activity and goal. Marble works out your targets from there — calories and macros you can actually hit, not a number pulled off a chart.

II

Take material away

Photograph what you eat. Marble identifies the dish, estimates calories and protein, carbs and fat, and tells you how confident it is — so you know when to trust it and when to correct it.

III

Refine the surface

Each week Marble looks at what you logged and what the scale did, then adjusts your targets. Your plan follows your body instead of the other way round.

Why it's different

Most trackers are built to be forgotten — snap, log, move on. Marble is built so that in three months you know more than when you started.

Every meal comes back with what's in it and why it lands where it does. Ask the coach anything about it. The goal isn't a longer logging streak; it's that eventually you can look at a plate and already know.
